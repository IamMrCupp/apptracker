Capture bookmarklet

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. On a job posting, click it — this tracker opens in a new tab with the entry form already filled in. Nothing is saved until you review it and hit Save entry.

What it reads

Most job boards publish machine-readable JobPosting markup, because Google for Jobs rewards it — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and a long tail of others. The bookmarklet reads that first, then falls back to the page title and address.

LinkedIn is the weak spot. Its job pages sit behind a login and publish no such markup, so expect the company and role to need correcting there.

Where your data goes

Nowhere new. The bookmarklet reads only the page you are already looking at and passes what it finds to as a normal link. No extension, no third-party service, no background access to your browsing.

If it does not open

Some sites set a Content-Security-Policy strict enough to block bookmarklets. Copy the posting details in by hand there — the tracker works the same either way.
